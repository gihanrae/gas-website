GAS

GROUND ASSULT SURVIVAL

You are an ex-military person placed into a military island along with 10 others to survive till last man standing. You observe, communicate, decide and take action — then every action has consequences.

// CORE DESIGN MOTIVE & INSPIRATION

"I believe there is a place for casual players in the mil-sim genre without removing tension and depth. GAS is inspired by CS:GO and PUBG along with tactical mil-sim titles like Squad and ARMA where you experience intense, heart-pumping firefights. Here players get freedom with a crucial purpose — their own survival — where they must overcome challenges and take decisions and live with the consequences. Features like full ballistic simulation, large hillside landscapes with compact POIs for both long-range and CQB combat, extensive in-match weapon customization allowing players to build a kit for their own playstyle, and most importantly, experiencing raw, unfiltered gunplay."

02 // GAME MODE

EXPERIENCE THE CONFLICT FOR SURVIVAL

10 operators deploy onto the island with zero safety nets. Every engagement is earned through reconnaissance, positioning, and split-second tactical calculations. Survival demands caution: gunshots echo across mountain ranges, and reloading a half-empty magazine leaves you vulnerable.

04 // ammunition

REALISTIC AMMO MANAGEMENT

Reloading doesn't merge loose rounds into an infinite pool. Individual magazines retain their exact round count. Check your mags, pack your bullets, and conserve ammunition when the circle tightens.

MILITARY ISLAND

A decommissioned island fortress with rugged ridges, dense forests, and abandoned military POIs—where snipers dominate the high ground and breachers fight through underground bunkers and radar stations.
